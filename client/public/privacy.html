Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

The Chekata mobile app ("the App") is an internal operations tool for The Chekata's hospitality and business management platform (CHAIMS). It is provided for use by authorized staff and management only, and is not intended for use by the general public.

Who this applies to

The App is restricted to individuals issued a login by The Chekata's system administrators. Access requires a valid staff account; there is no public sign-up.

Information we collect

  • Account information: username, full name, and the modules/permissions assigned to your account.
  • Operational data you enter or view: bookings, orders, approvals, staff records, and related business records tied to your role, stored on The Chekata's own servers.
  • Session data: a secure session token stored on your device (via iOS Keychain / Secure Store) to keep you signed in.
  • Device/technical data: standard technical information such as app version and crash diagnostics, used only to maintain the app.

How we use this information

Information is used solely to operate The Chekata's internal business functions: managing bookings and orders, processing approvals, running payroll and reporting, and controlling access by role. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Where data is stored

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections to The Chekata's own servers and is not shared with external analytics or advertising services.

Data retention

Operational records are retained in line with The Chekata's internal business and legal record-keeping requirements. Accounts can be deactivated at any time by an administrator.

Your rights

Staff members may request a copy of their account information or its deletion, subject to legitimate business record-keeping obligations, by contacting an administrator using the details below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to info@thechekata.com.
